Privacy Policy

Beacon is a marine conditions and hazard-reporting app for Florida boaters. This page says exactly what we collect, why, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

We do not track you. Beacon has no advertising, no analytics SDK, no third-party trackers, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers.

Your position is used on your phone. It picks your waterway, sorts marinas by distance and draws your boat on the chart. Beacon does not upload a continuous track of where you have been.

One exception, and it is deliberate: when you choose to file a hazard report, the coordinates of that hazard are sent with it and are visible to other boaters. That is the entire point of the report. Nothing else about your position leaves the phone.

Hazard reports — read this part

A hazard report is a public safety message. When you file one, these things are sent to our database and shown to other boaters:

  • What you reported and how serious you said it was
  • The coordinates of the hazard — either where your boat was, or the spot you pressed on the chart
  • The display name on your account
  • The time you filed it

Reports clear from the feed three hours after posting. If you do not want a position attached to a report, do not file one — there is no way to post a hazard warning that is useful without saying where it is.

Where your data goes

Beacon uses a small number of services, each for one job:

  • Supabase — our database and sign-in, hosted in the United States.
  • Apple — subscriptions, Sign in with Apple, and push notifications.
  • Resend — sends the sign-in code to your email address.
  • NOAA and the National Weather Service — we request conditions, tides and chart tiles for the waterway you are viewing. These are US government services; your account is not identified to them.
  • OpenStreetMap and OpenSeaMap — map tiles for the chart.

That is the complete list. There is no advertising network, no analytics product and no data broker in it.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your personal information.
  • Upload a background track of where your boat has been.
  • Use your location while Beacon is closed. Location access is “While Using the App”, and the live chart stops following you the moment you leave the app.
  • Ask for, store, or see your payment details.

Deleting your account and data

Open Beacon, go to Profile → Delete account & data. This permanently removes your name, email, boat profile, saved marinas and notification settings from our servers, and clears what Beacon has stored on your phone. It cannot be undone.

Two things deliberately survive, and you should know about both.

  • Hazard reports and tips you posted stay on the feed, with your name and boat name stripped off them. A warning about a submerged piling is useful to the next boater whether or not the person who filed it still has an account, so we unlink it rather than delete it. Reports expire from the feed three hours after posting anyway; tips stay.
  • The date this device first opened Beacon stays in the iOS Keychain, so the free 30 days cannot be restarted by reinstalling. It is a date on your phone and nothing else — no identifier, and it is never sent to us.

An App Store subscription is billed by Apple and is not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

If you cannot reach the app, email crew@beaconboating.app and we will delete your account on request.

Your rights

If you are in a jurisdiction with data-protection rights — including California (CCPA/CPRA) and the EU/UK (GDPR) — you may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email crew@beaconboating.app. We will respond within 30 days. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Beacon is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to your rows in our database is enforced at the database level, so one account cannot read another's profile. No system is perfect; if you find a weakness, please tell us at crew@beaconboating.app before telling anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, we will update the date at the top and say so in the app before the change takes effect.

Beacon is not a navigation system

This is a privacy policy, but it belongs here too: Beacon is a planning aid. Depths shift, markers move, and boater reports are unverified. Keep official charts and a working VHF aboard, and trust your eyes over any screen. In an emergency call 911 or hail the U.S. Coast Guard on VHF Channel 16.
